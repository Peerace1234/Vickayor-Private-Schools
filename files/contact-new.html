Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?

Visit our Admissions page to start your application online or contact our admissions team for guidance.

What is the expected response time?

We typically respond to all enquiries within 24 business hours. For urgent matters, please call us directly.

Can I schedule a campus visit?

Yes! Please select "Campus Visit Request" in the enquiry form, or message us on WhatsApp to arrange a convenient time.

How can I get information about school fees?

Contact our admissions team for a detailed fee breakdown. Fees vary by class level and are available upon request.

Is there a payment plan available?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. Contact the school office or admissions team for options.

What documents do I need for admission?

See our Admissions page for a complete list of required documents.
